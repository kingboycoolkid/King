Chapter one

A room where people talk to strangers for a living.

Akalade specialises in direct marketing and customer acquisition. The team is, in the company's own words, vibrant and high energy, and that is not a decoration: the work is face to face, on a floor, with people who came in to buy something else.

The company says you will not just find a job here, you will find a community that champions growth. That is a large claim. The next chapter is the one that has to earn it.

Chapter two

The job, before the good part.

The role is Account Manager. Akalade describes it as connecting with customers personally to provide tailored solutions. Here is what that actually asks of you, taken from the job as the company writes it.

  • 01 Presenting and promoting to prospective clients. In person, on a retail floor, to people who did not come in expecting you.
  • 02 Hitting sales targets, and carrying part of the team's. The number is visible. That cuts both ways, and it is the honest part of the job.
  • 03 Regular training sessions, not occasional ones. Akalade's own founder went from trainee to trainer here. Chapter five is that story.
  • 04 High school diploma or equivalent. Degree preferred, not required. What is required: communication, high energy, and responding fast when something is urgent.
This block needs Akalade's own words Pay structure goes here, in plain numbers: base, commission, and a realistic first year range. Say it in chapter two, before the page has asked the reader for anything. A candidate who finds the number here trusts the rest of the page. A candidate who finds it somewhere else does not.
